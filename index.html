Stickman Draw Run - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Stickman Draw Run ("the app") is a free game published by Ori Granot. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app stores

Your progress (scores, coins, unlocked skins, missions) is saved only on your device. There are no accounts, and we do not upload your progress anywhere.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and OS information, and ad interaction data. On iOS the app asks for permission (App Tracking Transparency) before using the advertising identifier for personalized ads; if you decline, you still get the full game with non-personalized ads. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or any content you draw. Ink strokes exist only in the running game.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with an updated date.
